# WG2 — API / SI dimensions / provenance

- `render_mouth_port_monitor(config, prescribed_flow_48k_m3_s, playback_full_scale_pa=8.0)` accepts a validated `PassiveWaveguideConfig` (still restricted static/equal-section-length) and finite 1D numpy/list source flow array with 2–24000 48k samples (max 0.5 seconds). It rejects clipping, invalid scale, nonfinite flow, and clocks outside the independently checked 60–75kHz receiver range. The first creator candidate deliberately restricts the geometry to **16 sections and 2 cells per section**, preserving M0/M1 while rejecting the general n=4/8 acoustic method at this observation fidelity; a combined work budget of 1,200,000 section-cells×ticks also applies.
- Every physical tick uses prescribed piecewise-linear `U(t)` sampled at tick midpoints in m³/s and returns exact `WaveguideStep`: `mouth_pressure_pa` and `mouth_flow_m3_s`. No post-WAV source gains.
- `MouthPortMonitorTake` stores immutable tuples `mouth_pressure_physics_pa` (Pa), `mouth_flow_physics_m3_s` (m³/s), `mouth_port_pressure_monitor_pa` (Pa at 48k, delayed), `wav_pcm16_mono_48k` (bytes). Scalar metadata: `physics_clock_hz`, `physics_ticks`, `output_samples`, fixed `playback_full_scale_pa`, `termination`, observer version and FIR group delay.
- Hash each raw input/pressure/monitor float64 array and WAV bytes separately. Dict `provenance()` is a **local numerical observation record**, not a complete immutable creator Take; it **does not contain** prepared body/revision/source-code commit. Those identifiers must come from the separately validated downstream backend adapter.
- Raw pressure unit Pa is not digital full scale. A *fixed* positive Pa/full scale maps preview pressure to PCM16 via rounding; excess pressure fails explicitly instead of clipping or silently normalizing every body/Takes to their own peak. Likewise, no ear-distance, listener or microphone calibration is inferred.
- Energy: `max_abs_energy_residual_j`, `summed_source_work_j`, `summed_outlet_dissipation_j`, `final_stored_energy_j` are native physics quantities; the anti-alias output is not an acoustic energy recomputation.
- The caller must not label a preliminary mouth-pressure recording as external far-field sound. Body dynamics/ASMR and opt-in motor planning remain upstream future capabilities.

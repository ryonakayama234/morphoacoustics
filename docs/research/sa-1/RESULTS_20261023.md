## Causal interpretation and limits
The frozen source's very small *absolute* stochastic flow still carries an appreciable high-band spectral distribution as a residual component. The passive tract has frequency-selective amplification of **volume-flow transfer** (source highband ~0.00128% → ideal lip highband ~0.0551%). The explicitly modeled free-field **monopole observation factor scales with frequency `|R_obs(f)| ∝ f`**, and applying this declared law to that signal shifts the modeled observer's fractional spectral power toward high frequencies (~84.76%; actual OLA ~80.76%).

Therefore **JET-P0 did NOT fail because it failed to render physics, nor because frame overlap-add is globally nonlinear, nor because the tube demonstrably creates energy**. Its candidate stochastic source is inappropriate **under the current source-port/tract/radiation combination** for the prospectively frozen acoustic quality screen.

**Important distinction:** frequency-proportional monopole radiation gain is a mathematically/physically expected approximation. SA-1 does not prove its sign or form to be an outright bug. But the present termination `Z_L=0.05 Z_c` is an empirical constant resistor, whereas observation is a separately specified free-field point monopole. The system has NOT established a mutually consistent finite source–load and frequency-dependent lip-radiation boundary for dynamically coupled VF-1. The diagnoses of numerical mechanisms are strong; **physical boundary validity, biological jet-noise calibration, and auditory acceptability remain unverified**.

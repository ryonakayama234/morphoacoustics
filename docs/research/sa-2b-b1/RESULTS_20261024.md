### Important new limitation discovered on numerical causality
The implicit-midpoint *global* state solve is time-causal (uses no future input), energy consistent, and phase-convergent at specified frequencies, **but its transition matrix is dense**. Thus its discrete numerical domain of dependence is not strictly finite-speed: a very small pre-arrival pressure can occur at the far boundary. From the archived B0-case seeded broadband drive, there is a remote lip-pressure value about 2e-16 Pa at sample zero and the |p_lip| threshold 1e-6 of its peak is exceeded by sample ten (versus ~22.39 samples continuous one-way physical propagation). Because the source lasts 128 samples and the threshold is arbitrary, do not mistake this diagnostic for a measured acoustic group velocity; rather it is a **counterexample to claiming exact finite-speed numerical wavefront propagation** from the current implicit solver.

Therefore this implementation remains a **valuable verified A1 energy/transfer prototype**, but the broader Issue #177 finite-speed wavefront and group-delay scientific gate is **HOLD/PENDING**. Before calling B1 fully verified, compare against a *local* passive travelling-wave/fractional-delay model or demonstrably energy-consistent explicit staggered solver with a frozen empirical wavefront/group-delay oracle and source-independent passivity checks. No arbitrary EQ, artificial source, or silent anatomical delay rounding is acceptable.

## Frozen physical equations
For each serial section i: Z_i = rho*c/S_i (pressure/volume-flow impedance), and cell physical travel dt=dx/c. At an ideal ZERO-volume junction between left and right sections with incoming pressure waves a_L (toward lip) and b_R (toward source), and outgoing b_L and a_R, impose exact p_L=p_R and U_L=U_R:
    b_L = (Z_R-Z_L)/(Z_L+Z_R)*a_L + 2*Z_L/(Z_L+Z_R)*b_R
    a_R = 2*Z_R/(Z_L+Z_R)*a_L + (Z_L-Z_R)/(Z_L+Z_R)*b_R
Hence (a_L²-b_L²)/Z_L = (a_R²-b_R²)/Z_R, unitary in power-normalized pressure waves. Check the equations by a completely independent linear solve for p_j, U_j rather than only reuse these formulas.
All wave delay FIFOs store actual traveling pressures; their physical energy is dt * sum(a_i²+b_i²)/Z_i. Adjacent sections exchange equal and opposite instantaneous per-step energy. Source boundary is ACTIVE prescribed U_in and signed W=dt*(a_s²-b_s²)/Z_0=dt*p_s U_in. Physical lip terminates with original B0 BaffledRadiationOnePort at the **actual last-section lip area**, same rho,c and common internal physics sample rate.

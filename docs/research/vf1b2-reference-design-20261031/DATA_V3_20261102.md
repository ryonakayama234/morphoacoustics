## Normative research entities (schemas are internal candidates)
1. StudyManifest: study_id, protocol_version, UTC/JST time, Core source SHA and tree, exact experimental ancestor SHAs and branch-status, Python/NumPy/solver packages, CPU/OS FP context, verified independent code hashes, experiment rights, external references, hashes and reviewers. Source pinning is traceability, not an authentication proof.
2. PhysicalFixtureRevision: tissue {mL,mU kg; kL,kU,kc N/m; dL,dU N*s/m; x0[m],v0[m/s]; external abs pressure Pa}, glottal {Lg,ell,h0(z),A_floor, mesh reference}, fluid {rho_ref kg/m3, P_atm Pa abs, gamma, EOS_id, loss_id, separation_id, valid Regime/Re/pressure}, reservoir boundary {up/down abs pressure, T/rho or EOS-consistent state, incoming characteristics and timing}, and named geometry reference.
3. SimulationRequest: exact fixture digest; physical actuation pressure schedule Psub_abs(t) and cessation, no periodic prescribed source/F0; initial full fluid/tissue fields; dt selection method, quadrature, N, nonlinear residual limits, duration, revision and applicable capability envelope.
4. RawPhysicsTrace: time s and temporal placement, xL/xU m, vL/vU m/s, h/A m/m2 along z, rho kg/m3, p_abs Pa, p_ext Pa abs, local u m/s and volume flow U m3/s, face mdot kg/s, momentum flux N, total enthalpy flux W, apparent temperature/entropy assumption, and any contact/flow regime flags. Distinguish numerical model predictions from measurements.
5. IndependentLedger (separately implemented): fluid/tissue energies J; boundary total-enthalpy flux W; signed pressure-wall work W; external pressure-work W; physical fluid loss, tissue damping J/W; change in stored mass kg; pointwise and integrated conservation residuals; near-zero denominator floor with provenance.
6. ReferenceComparison: external source PMID/DOI/page/figure/data license, source SHA if bytes accessible, model-vs-reference geometry/coordinate map, measurand and observer location, pressure reference/gauge, estimated uncertainty and representativeness, excluded conditions, calibration, any independent blind/holdout status.
7. Verdict: code/numerical/model/physiology/phonation/creator evaluations stored separately, each test IDs plus evidence hashes and reason. Refusal and unknown are valid results.
8. Future CreativeTake (NOT created by this work): immutable actual acoustic observation plus exact BodyRevision/gesture/scene/solver/source/receiver refs. NEVER a mislabeled raw p/U trace or the original B0 synthetic fixture.

## Measurement plan: operational definitions
| Quantity | Directly computed or observed | Why measured | Dangerous alias / prohibited shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rho A and integrated mass [kg] | cell conservative state / independent integration | changing-volume continuity | forgetting swept fluid volume |
| p_abs(z), p_abs-p_ext [Pa] | fluid field / gauge reference | tissue force sign and shape | treating inlet p as wall pressure |
| F_L,F_U [N] | wall pressure integral using declared shape basis | modal asymmetry | using scalar mean pressure as two force measurements |
| x_i,v_i and modal projections [m,m/s] | mechanical state at fixed point in physical time | oscillation/phase/onset/decay | phase inferred from raw mean displacement |
| U(z) [m3/s], mdot(z) [kg/s] | face state | airflow and flux continuity | treating total U as acoustic fluctuation U' |
| E_fluid,E_tissue [J] | independently integrated primary states | possible stored energy | cumulative work without energy accounting |
| Phi_in/out [W], P_wall [W], P_ext dV/dt [W] | raw face and wall states | distinguish physical power transfer | pressure-only pU as full energy |
| S_loss and tissue dissipation [W] | specified nonnegative modeled sinks | ensure genuine external energy causes growth | injecting negative intrinsic damping |
| acoustic p', U', E_ac [Pa,m3/s,J] | C2 only, explicitly named plane | tract feedback, passivity | p_mouth or p_in as air-at-ear |
| output y_receiver(t) [Pa] | E only with qualified radiation/receiver | audible quality | calling uncalibrated proxy a human ear signal |
| F0/regime, spectral envelope | D/E only after causal source supported | oscillation/acoustic structure | tuning F0/WAV to sound prettier |

## Calculation and observation cadence
Physical solver integration step dt_phys selected on scales c, |u|, Lg/N and accuracy; each snapshot has actual step/time index and residual. Internal observer stores a pre-registered subsampling schedule that preserves extrema, event/onset windows, and integral flux history. Future audio observation dt_audio may be 1/48000 s but must derive from physical state by a declared, converged observer/radiation operator, not by arbitrary EQ/time-stretch/voice replacement. Source field values and downstream waveform are different record types.

## Uncertainty and independent observer policy
- Dimensional tolerances: separate absolute errors in kg, J, N, Pa, m/s from normalized errors; no Euclidean norm mixing different units.
- Numeric uncertainties: discretization dt/dz, nonlinear solve residual, FP/replay behavior, quadrature; record convergence trend and floors. Different machines need not reproduce binary-identical floats; exact source/input hashes and allowed numerical equivalence are separate from saved-byte integrity.
- External uncertainties: reference geometry and pressure tap mapping, source acquisition rights/bytes, instrument gauge, calibration and data variability. Analytical/oracle matches cannot substitute for missing physiological measurements.
- Power residual: R_E = Delta(Ef+Et) - (W_in-W_out-W_ext-W_fluidloss-W_tissuedamp), each defined on the SAME time interval. Report absolute |R_E| [J] and ratio |R_E|/max(E_exchanged, E_floor), with the denominator and E_floor fixed independently before evaluation. The observer must not invoke the solver's own energy routine.
- Mass residual: R_M=Delta(m_total)-(int mdot_in dt-int mdot_out dt), report kg and normalized with an independently declared mass floor.
- Mode work: on a preregistered **complete cycle** (not arbitrary windows), W_i^cycle=int F_i v_i dt and D_i=int d_i v_i² dt; disclose coupling energy changes. If no robust cycles, report NOT_DEFINED rather than a positive net-work claim.
- A non-contact regime cannot be called closed-glottis phonation, and a steady-flow profile cannot be called a moving-wall pressure measurement.
- No pseudo-independent reviewers or fictional heldout biological samples; solo model development is legitimate but labels must identify any self-curated/derived reference.

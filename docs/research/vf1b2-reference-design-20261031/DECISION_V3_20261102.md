# VF-1B2 / Design decision V3 — physically driven phonator, bounded research architecture
Date: 2026-11-02 JST. Status: RECOMMENDED FINAL DESIGN FOR CREATOR REVIEW; NOT A3 PREREGISTRATION, NOT ENGINE IMPLEMENTATION APPROVAL, NOT PUBLIC ABI, NOT A VOICE RELEASE. Additive to PR #259 V1/V2; do not rewrite historical designs or PR #262 fixed-wall preregistration.

## Executive decision
Primary workstream = Core / Physical Execution / VF-1B2 research-local moving-wall glottal fluid–tissue coupling. Creative destination = a Character organ whose geometry and material, together with a task-level respiratory actuation, determine whether physical oscillation begins, sustains, changes under load, or fails. The actual sound and Studio usability are separate milestones. Keep OD-3 as an immutable Research Baseline for 16-cell prepared body × coordination, not a production-quality voice.

Choose:
1. A two-coordinate lower/upper moving-wall tissue model with positive stiffness and nonnegative intrinsic damping. A physically interpretable gap field spans the glottis; the two coordinates must be independently forced by spatial wall pressure.
2. A moving-area quasi-one-dimensional compressible continuum flow described by mass, axial momentum and a compatible thermodynamic energy balance. An ideal-gas/isentrope is first a MATHEMATICAL closure, not glottal biological validation; if viscous dissipation is added, heat/entropy bookkeeping must be resolved or the approximation explicitly labeled.
3. Stage C1: tissue + fluid + upstream/downstream physical reservoirs; no claim about voice, tract feedback or realistic high-drive separation.
4. Stage C2 CONDITIONAL: attach a passive time-domain tract only through a separately derived mean-flow/acoustic perturbation bridge, conserving the appropriate exchange; no substitution of absolute pressure/whole U for an acoustic p'/u' port.
5. Stage D: test autonomous onset/cessation, load intervention and two physically identified organ variants only after C1 and C2 gates; Stage E: distinct acoustic radiation and creator-quality acceptance.

## Choices rejected, and why
- Prescribed LF, direct pitch shifts, gain/EQ, splice, black-box TTS as the physical source: causal identity would be broken; retain LF only as a negative/control baseline.
- Reuse the VF-1A scalar valve and its former moving-volume adjustment while adding spatial wall forces: without a fresh complete fluid ledger risks double-counted wall work.
- Treat low-Re Poiseuille/lubrication PR #262 as valid at 800 Pa: unsupported extrapolation.
- Immediate source-to-SFI-3 p'U' connection: mean mass/enthalpy transport and acoustic second-order energy cannot be ignored.
- A full 3D FSI implementation now: cost and observability exceed the minimum falsifiable question. Use external 3D/measured references to challenge a reduced model.
- New body registry, production OrganGraph API, Studio tab/panel, SMC/ASMR integration: separate domain decision; no implementation authority in this design.

## Phase gates, without manufacturing a success
- A0: inventory exact SHA, source/interface/reference planes, ancestor test and review state; main is not to import stacked Drafts.
- A1: independently audit conservative continuum and discrete wall-power/mass/energy identities, signs, geometric conservation and reservoir conditions. If unclosed: STOP_MODEL_LAW.
- A2: independently qualify the mathematical EOS and fixed-wall analytic reference; record high-drive separation validity explicitly UNKNOWN. If no external measured/3D high-drive reference, allow low-domain mathematical C1 but G2 PHYSIOLOGY_HOLD and no biological extrapolation.
- A3: prospectively freeze fixture/time-space convergence regime, dimensions/units, numerical floors and falsifiers BEFORE C1 source code or results; only then B2a READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION.
- C1: noncontact, reservoir-coupled conservative moving-wall computation; physical source remains unadopted.
- C2: independently verified finite-flow/linear-acoustic bridge, same-step feedback and power accounting; if unavailable STOP_SOURCE_TRACT_COUPLING.
- D: preregister true autonomous oscillation/onset/cessation and body/load interventions; if absent G3 NO_SELF_OSCILLATION_FOUND, not a tuned alternative.
- E: source radiation/receiver and blinded same-task sound evidence, separately requiring creator GO for usable voice; never equate mouth-port pressure with ear sound.

## Release terminology
- Research Baseline: OD-3 existing model + exact simulation evidence, immutable reference.
- Research Candidate: VF-1B2 V3 physical model family, NOT an executable implementation.
- Validated Limited Mechanism: G0/G1 + scoped G2/G3 after independent scientific evidence, not necessarily speech.
- Creator-qualified voice release: separately qualified transmitted sound and creative workflow G4.
No stable Core public API, Studio Take, or main merge follows merely from accepting this design.

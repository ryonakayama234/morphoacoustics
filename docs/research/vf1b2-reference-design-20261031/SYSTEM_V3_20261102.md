## Pressure forces, absolute versus gauge
Fluid thermodynamic p_abs(z,t)>0 [Pa], external tissue p_ext [Pa abs]. Generalized force:
F_i = integral_0^Lg (p_abs-p_ext) (partial A/partial x_i) dz [N].
Wall/tissue power P_tissue_from_fluid=F_L v_L+F_U v_U [W].
Fluid wall pressure power uses absolute p_abs: -integral p_abs partial_t A dz. The two contributions do NOT cancel to zero by themselves: the remainder is external tissue pressure work -p_ext dV/dt. Include that external reservoir work in the TOTAL system ledger (or derive an equivalent consistent relative energy reference). Do not omit it by choosing convenient gauge.

Independent analytic force oracle: for (p_abs-p_ext)=p0+a z/Lg,
F_L=ell Lg(p0+a/3), F_U=ell Lg(p0+2a/3), and F_L-F_U=-ell Lg a/3.
Uniform gauge pressure a=0 gives no antisymmetric direct drive. This says nothing by itself about sustained oscillation.

## Compressible fluid: conservative quasi-1D mathematical skeleton
State rho(z,t)>0 [kg/m3], u(z,t) [m/s], p_abs [Pa], total cell fluid storage rho A(e+u^2/2).
- mass: partial_t(rho A)+partial_z(rho u A)=0;
- momentum: partial_t(rho u A)+partial_z[(rho u^2+p_abs)A]=p_abs partial_z A + S_m;
- energy: partial_t[rho A(e+u^2/2)]+partial_z[ U(rho e+rho u^2/2+p_abs)]=-p_abs partial_t A + S_E.
Here U=A u [m3/s]. Flux includes mass advection, kinetic energy, internal energy and pressure enthalpy; pressure*volume-flow alone is NOT the full moving-flow energy flux. S_m and S_E are separate dimensionally consistent per-axial-length source terms. In an inviscid/no-separation mathematical baseline both vanish.

First EOS candidate: reversible isentropic ideal-gas barotrope p_abs=P_atm (rho/rho_atm)^gamma, e(rho)=p_abs/[(gamma-1)rho] (+ fixed reference), gamma>1. This is NOT a measured laryngeal closure. If a viscous drag or separation loss is proposed, irreversible heating/entropy storage versus untracked heat rejection must be explicitly reconciled. Do not combine barotropic reversible pressure with an ad hoc negative energy sink and call it fully thermodynamic. Either use a separately verified compatible dissipative closure or declare a limited mechanical-energy approximation.
